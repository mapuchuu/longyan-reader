Works where you read.

Safari, Chrome, Mail, Notes, and most other apps. Highlight some Chinese, click the eye, and the card opens right next to the text.

Made for reading.

The meanings come from CC-CEDICT, a free dictionary of nearly 200,000 words. A model on your Mac picks the one the sentence means.

Privacy & Security
Security
Allow applications fromApp Store & Known Developers
“Longyan Reader” was blocked to protect your Mac.Open Anyway
  1. Open the zip and drag Longyan Reader to Applications.
  2. Open it. macOS says it can’t verify the app. Click Done.
  3. In System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.
  4. Turn on Accessibility when the app asks, and download the reading model.

macOS asks because the app isn’t signed with a paid Apple developer account. You only do this once. Updates install on their own.

Free, and open source.

No account, no subscription. The code is on GitHub under the MIT license.
