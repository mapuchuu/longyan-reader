The first full release. Highlight Chinese in any app with a drag or a double-click, then click the eye that appears next to it, or press ⌃⌥L. Each word shows the meaning it has in that sentence, with pinyin and the other meanings a click away.

**New**

- Report a Problem in the menu opens a GitHub issue with your Mac's details filled in. Nothing is sent until you submit it.
- If the reading model is damaged, the menu offers to download it again.

**Better**

- Translations of part of a sentence stay closer to what you highlighted.
- Traditional characters get the same notes as simplified ones.
- Numbers stay in a selection: 2023年 is looked up whole.
- Clearer messages when the reading model is missing or fails to download. Downloads recover from bad connections.
- Exported spreadsheets open in Excel with the Chinese intact.

**Fixed**

- Phrase cards no longer drop every word after an emoji.
- Some words showed a pointer to another entry instead of a meaning.
- Crashes when quitting during a lookup and on a full disk.
- A password copied from a password manager no longer comes back to the clipboard after a lookup.
- History no longer loses words or breaks on a damaged file.

Needs an Apple silicon Mac with macOS 15 or later.
