The first full release.

- Highlight Chinese in any app, then click the eye next to it or press ⌃⌥L.
- Each word shows the meaning it has in that sentence, with pinyin and the other meanings a click away.
- Translations of part of a sentence stay closer to what you highlighted.
- Clearer messages when the reading model is missing, won't open, or a download fails, and the download recovers from bad connections.
- Exported spreadsheets open in Excel with the Chinese intact.
- Fixes for rare crashes on a full disk and when quitting during a lookup.

Needs an Apple silicon Mac with macOS 15 or later.
