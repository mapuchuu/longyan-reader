## Notes

- **分配** [fēn pèi] *to assign* · cultural context  
  To assign. Until the 1990s the state assigned graduates to jobs, and older people still remember waiting for their 分配 and where it sent them.
- **不了** [bù le] *no thanks (used to politely but informally decline)* · lost in translation  
  No thanks, said lightly. It's softer than 不要, and you use it to turn down a second helping without any fuss.
- **不错** [bù cuò] *not bad* · lost in translation  
  Not bad, and it means good. You say 不错 as real praise, because saying more would sound like too much.
- **矛盾** [máo dùn] *contradictory* · lost in translation  
  Torn inside. 我很矛盾 means you want two opposite things and can't choose between them.
- **高级** [gāo jí] *high grade* · lost in translation  
  Classy, fancy. 看起来很高级 is what you say when something looks expensive and tasteful, and now you hear 高级感 everywhere.
- **妇女** [fù nǚ] *woman* · cultural context  
  A woman, in official language. You see it in 妇女节 and 妇联, and a young woman may not like being called one because it sounds middle-aged.
- **发达** [fā dá] *(literary) to achieve fame and fortune; to prosper* · lost in translation  
  To make it big. 等我发达了 is how you joke about the day you're rich, and you'd remember your friends then.
- **空军** [kōng jūn] *(slang) to fail to catch anything (on a fishing trip); to get skunked* · lost in translation  
  To go home with no fish. Anglers say 今天又空军了 and laugh about it, because it happens to everyone.
- **亲自** [qīn zì] *personally* · lost in translation  
  In person, and it raises the person. 领导亲自来了 sounds like an honor, so saying 我亲自做的 about your own cooking is a little joke.
- **实践** [shí jiàn] *practice* · cultural context  
  Practice, as opposed to theory. 实践是检验真理的唯一标准 is a 1978 slogan you learned at school, and it's tied to the start of the reform years.
- **人士** [rén shì] *person* · lost in translation  
  A person, in formal writing. 知情人士 is how a report cites an unnamed insider, and you know not to expect a name.
- **帝国主义** [dì guó zhǔ yì] *imperialism* · cultural context  
  Imperialism. You learned it in history class as the force behind China's century of humiliation, so it has a hard edge.
