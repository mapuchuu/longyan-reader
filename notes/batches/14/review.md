## Notes

- **书记** [shū ji] *secretary (chief official of a branch of a socialist or communist party)* · cultural context  
  The Party secretary. In a city or a university the 书记 outranks the mayor or president, and you know that's who really decides.
- **哪里** [nǎ lǐ] *humble expression denying compliment* · lost in translation  
  Not at all, said to a compliment. You answer 哪里哪里 because taking praise outright feels immodest.
- **真是** [zhēn shi] *(coll.) (used to express disapproval, annoyance etc)* · lost in translation  
  Honestly, said in annoyance. You mutter 你这人真是 or 真是的 and let it trail off, and everyone hears the complaint.
- **手段** [shǒu duàn] *trick; wile* · lost in translation  
  A trick or a ruthless move. Someone 有手段 knows how to get what they want, and you say it with part admiration and part warning.
- **封建** [fēng jiàn] *feudalistic* · lost in translation  
  Backward, stuck in old ideas. You tell someone 你太封建了 when they have old-fashioned views on marriage or women, half teasing and half serious.
- **当年** [dāng nián] *in those days; back then; in those years* · lost in translation  
  Back then, in your younger days. 想当年 is how older people start a story, and you know you're about to hear how things used to be.
- **文章** [wén zhāng] *hidden meaning* · lost in translation  
  A hidden meaning. 这里面有文章 means something is going on behind the scenes, and you start reading between the lines.
