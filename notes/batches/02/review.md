- **补贴** [bǔ tiē] *allowance* · cultural context  
  Extra money paid on top of your base salary, for meals, housing or transport. When you compare two jobs you ask about 补贴 straight away, because it can make up a real share of what you take home.
- **专政** [zhuān zhèng] *dictatorship* · cultural context  
  Rule by one class, enforced through state power. You know it from 无产阶级专政 in politics class, and it sounds like official doctrine rather than an insult.
- **主动** [zhǔ dòng] *to take the initiative* · lost in translation  
  To make the first move without waiting to be asked. You hear it as praise at work and as courage in romance: 你主动一点 nudges you to speak first.
- **人缘** [rén yuán] *the quality of one's relationships with other people (e.g. 人緣好|人缘好[ren2 yuan2 hao3] "to be well liked")* · lost in translation  
  How much people warm to you and want you around. If your 人缘好, colleagues help you unasked and take your side, and you can't get that by trying too hard.
- **体会** [tǐ huì] *to know from experience* · lost in translation  
  To really grasp something by living through it. You hear 体, the body, in it: being told is one thing, but 亲身体会 means you've felt it yourself.
- **劳动** [láo dòng] *physical labor* · cultural context  
  Work, usually with your hands, spoken of as something honourable. You grew up with the slogan 劳动最光荣 and with school chores called 劳动, so it sounds weightier than plain work.
