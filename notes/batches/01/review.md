## Notes

- **紧张** [jǐn zhāng] *intense* · lost in translation  
  Busy and under pressure, with no slack. When your work is 紧张, you picture a packed schedule and no room to breathe, not stage fright.
- **紧张** [jǐn zhāng] *in short supply* · lost in translation  
  In short supply, as if stretched tight. When housing or money is 紧张, you feel the squeeze: there isn't enough and everyone is scrambling.
- **宽容** [kuān róng] *tolerant* · lost in translation  
  Willing to let other people's faults and differences pass. You feel the width in 宽: a roomy heart that doesn't keep score, warmer than simply putting up with someone.
- **比较** [bǐ jiào] *comparatively* · lost in translation  
  Fairly or rather, with nothing actually being compared. When you say 今天比较忙, you are gently saying it's pretty busy, a modest way to put it.
- **应聘** [yìng pìn] *to apply for an advertised position* · lost in translation  
  To answer a job posting by applying. You're responding to an employer's call, their 招聘 and your 应聘, and nothing is settled yet.
- **试探** [shì tàn] *to sound out* · lost in translation

- **赡养** [shàn yǎng] *to support* · cultural context  
  To provide for ageing parents or other dependants. You hear duty in it: grown children owe their parents a living, and the law says so too.
- **负责** [fù zé] *conscientious* · lost in translation  
  Dependable about whatever is yours to do. Calling a doctor or colleague 负责 is real praise: you trust them to see it through and answer for it.
- **肯定** [kěn dìng] *to give recognition; to affirm* · lost in translation  
  To recognise that someone's work counts. You picture a boss or official saying your effort was right and worthwhile, which feels like being formally seen.
- **翘楚** [qiáo chǔ] *person of outstanding talent* · lost in translation  
  Someone who stands out above everyone in their field. You picture the tallest shrub rising over the rest, and it's polished praise for a speech or profile, not for chatting.
- **退休金** [tuì xiū jīn] *retirement pension; pension payments received after retirement* · cultural context  
  The monthly pension paid after you retire. Talk of it quickly turns to who worked for the state and who didn't, because you know the gap can be huge.
- **师父** [shī fu] *master* · cultural context  
  A master who teaches you a craft, martial art or monastic path. You hear 父, father, in it: the bond is close and lasting, and you owe them loyalty almost as you would a parent.
- **单元** [dān yuán] *(in a residential building) entrance or staircase* · cultural context  
  One entrance and stairwell of an apartment block. You say 三号楼二单元 as part of your address, because each block is split into these, each with its own door and neighbours.
