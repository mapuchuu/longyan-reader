- **小区** [xiǎo qū] *housing estate; community; neighborhood* · cultural context  
  A gated residential compound of apartment blocks. You picture the guard at the gate, shared courtyards and old people on benches, a more enclosed world than a neighbourhood.
- **底层** [dǐ céng] *lowest level; lowest rung (of society etc)* · cultural context  
  The very bottom of society or a company. 底层 sounds heavy: you picture migrant workers and long hours, and 从底层做起 means you earned your way up.
