## Notes

- **动手** [dòng shǒu] *to raise a hand to hit sb* · lost in translation  
  To start hitting someone. When you hear 是他先动手的, you know a quarrel has crossed the line, and everyone wants to establish who swung first.
- **卖力** [mài lì] *to work hard* · lost in translation  
  To put visible, all-out effort into a task. You hear 卖, to sell, in it: you're spending your strength, sometimes with one eye on who's watching.
- **卫生** [wèi shēng] *hygiene; sanitation* · cultural context  
  Cleanliness, and in daily life the cleaning itself. When you 搞卫生 you're mopping and scrubbing, and you may remember being assigned classroom duty for it at school.
- **合情合理** [hé qíng hé lǐ] *(idiom) reasonable; fair* · lost in translation  
  Fair by both feeling and logic. You feel it satisfies 情 and 理 together, so it's considerate of people as well as correct by the rules.
- **向往** [xiàng wǎng] *to yearn for* · lost in translation  
  To long for a place or life you don't have yet. You picture gazing toward it from far away, more wistful and hopeful than simply wanting it.
- **器重** [qì zhòng] *to regard sth as valuable; to think highly of (a younger person, a subordinate etc)* · lost in translation  
  To value a junior and trust them with more. You feel marked out from above: only a superior can 器重 you, and it hints at bigger things coming.
- **基层** [jī céng] *grassroots unit* · cultural context  
  The lowest rung of an organisation, where policy meets real people. You hear 下基层 and picture officials sent out to villages and neighbourhoods, and it's where careers are said to be proven.
- **处理** [chǔ lǐ] *to punish* · lost in translation  
  To discipline someone. 被处理 sounds vague and ominous: you picture a quiet punishment from your organisation, with nobody saying exactly what happened.
- **外人** [wài rén] *outsider* · lost in translation  
  Someone outside your circle. When you say 别把我当外人, you're asking to be treated like family, because the line between inside and outside matters so much.
- **外包** [wài bāo] *outsourcing* · cultural context  
  Work contracted out to another company. In a Chinese office, you know a 外包 worker can sit next to you with a different badge, lower pay and lower standing.
- **大姐** [dà jiě] *older sister (also polite term of address for a girl or woman slightly older than the speaker)* · cultural context  
  A friendly, respectful way to address a woman a bit older than you. You'd call a stranger at the market 大姐, and it makes you sound down to earth, though some women would rather not hear it.
- **埋头** [mái tóu] *to immerse oneself in* · lost in translation  
  To bury yourself in a task, head down. 埋头苦干 is quiet, uncomplaining hard work, the kind you're raised to admire.
- **半天** [bàn tiān] *a long time* · lost in translation  
  A long while, however long it really was. When you say 等了半天, it may be twenty minutes, and you're complaining more than measuring.
- **反正** [fǎn zhèng] *anyway* · lost in translation  
  Anyway, with a small shrug. When you say 反正我也没事, you mean things come out the same whatever happens, so there's no point fussing.
