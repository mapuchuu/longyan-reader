## Notes

- **运动** [yùn dòng] *campaign* · cultural context  
  A political campaign. If you lived through the Mao years, 运动 on its own means the next round of denunciations, and hearing one is coming makes you uneasy.
- **咱们** [zán men] *we or us (including both the speaker and the person(s) spoken to)* · lost in translation  
  We, and it includes you. When I say 咱们 I pull you in with me, so it feels warmer than 我们, which could leave you out.
- **咱们** [zán men] *(dialect) (in a coaxing or familiar way) you* · lost in translation  
  You, said fondly. Saying 咱们不哭 to a crying child softens it, because it sounds like we're getting through this together.
- **原来** [yuán lái] *so, actually, as it turns out* · lost in translation  
  So that's it. You say 原来 the moment something clicks, with a small feeling of finding out what was true all along.
- **分别** [fēn bié] *to part; to leave each other* · lost in translation  
  To part, often for a long time. It feels heavier than saying goodbye, and you use it for people you may not see for years.
- **先进** [xiān jìn] *meritorious; exemplary (deeds etc)* · cultural context  
  Model, award-worthy. 先进工作者 is an official workplace honor, and you picture a certificate and a red banner.
- **中央** [zhōng yāng] *central authorities (of a state)* · cultural context  
  The top leadership in Beijing. When you hear 中央 on its own you think of the Party Central Committee, and a document from it feels final.
- **人大** [rén dà] *people's congress, a legislative body within the PRC's system of government at any of various levels – national, provincial, municipal, county or township (abbr. for 人民代表大會|人民代表大会[ren2 min2 dai4 biao3 da4 hui4])* · cultural context  
  The people's congress, China's legislature. You picture the Great Hall of the People each March, and you don't expect a surprise vote.
- **思想** [sī xiǎng] *ideology* · cultural context  
  Your way of thinking, and your politics. When someone does your 思想工作 they're talking you round, at home as much as at work.
- **形式** [xíng shì] *formality* · lost in translation  
  A formality, something done for show. With 走形式 you go through the motions, and everyone in the room knows it.
- **理论** [lǐ lùn] *to argue* · lost in translation  
  To argue it out and demand fairness. 找他理论 means you march over to have it out with someone who wronged you.
- **科学** [kē xué] *rational* · lost in translation  
  Sensible, done properly. When you say 这不科学 you're half joking, meaning this just makes no sense.
- **精神** [jīng shen] *vigor; vitality* · lost in translation  
  Lively, sharp, well turned out. You say 真精神 to a young man with a new haircut, and it's about the whole look of being awake and alive.
- **结果** [jié guǒ] *in the end; as a result* · lost in translation  
  In the end, and often not how you hoped. In a story 结果 warns you of the letdown, as in 我等了一天，结果他没来.
- **服务** [fú wù] *to serve* · cultural context  
  To serve. You see 为人民服务 on signs at government offices, and depending on the day at the counter you read it straight or with a sigh.
