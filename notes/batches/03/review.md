## Notes

- **付出** [fù chū] *to pay; to expend; to invest (energy or time)* · lost in translation  
  To give your time and effort as something that costs you. When you say 我付出了那么多, you're keeping a quiet tally and feeling that something is owed back.
- **梨园** [lí yuán] *the world of Chinese opera* · cultural context  
  The world of traditional Chinese opera, named after a Tang emperor's troupe in a pear garden. You hear old stages, painted faces and performers trained from childhood.
- **沟通** [gōu tōng] *to communicate* · lost in translation  
  To talk something through until both sides understand each other. At work, 我们沟通一下 can sound friendly, and you may also hear a polite warning that something needs sorting out.
- **爽快** [shuǎng kuai] *frank and straightforward* · lost in translation  
  Quick and frank, with no fuss or haggling. When someone agrees 爽快, you feel the relief of not having to ask twice.
- **福利** [fú lì] *benefit (provided by an employer or organization)* · cultural context  
  The perks an employer gives beyond pay, like insurance, meals and holiday gift boxes. You ask about 福利 when weighing a job, and a company's mooncakes at Mid-Autumn count too.
- **经理** [jīng lǐ] *manager* · cultural context  
  A manager, though the title is handed out freely in China. When a 客户经理 calls you, you assume a salesperson, and they may manage nobody at all.
- **自尊心** [zì zūn xīn] *self-esteem* · lost in translation  
  Your pride, and how easily it bruises. Saying someone's 自尊心强 usually means they can't bear being looked down on, not that they feel good about themselves.
- **自由散漫** [zì yóu sǎn màn] *easygoing* · lost in translation  
  Undisciplined and unwilling to follow the rules. You hear a teacher or a boss writing you up, so it's a criticism and never praise for a free spirit.
- **自觉** [zì jué] *responsible; predisposed to do the right thing; considerate of others* · cultural context  
  Doing the right thing with nobody watching. You see signs saying 请自觉排队, and you feel the public expectation that you should police yourself.
- **解气** [jiě qì] *gratifying (esp. to see sb get their comeuppance)* · lost in translation  
  Satisfying because someone finally got what they deserved. You feel pent-up anger drain away, as if you had let out a long breath.
- **一下** [yī xià] *(after a verb) a bit; a little (indicating brief duration, or softening the tone, or suggesting giving sth a try)* · lost in translation  
  Just briefly, a little, added after a verb to soften it. When you say 帮我看一下, you make your request feel small and easy to say yes to.
