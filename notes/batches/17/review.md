## Notes

- **现实** [xiàn shí] *realistic; pragmatic; materialistic; self-interested* · lost in translation  
  Practical about money and status. 她很现实 means she chooses by what you can offer, and it's a mild criticism.
- **日子** [rì zi] *days of one's life* · lost in translation  
  The days of your life. 过日子 means simply living and getting by together, and it's how couples describe a marriage.
- **态度** [tài du] *attitude* · lost in translation  
  Attitude, often a bad one. 你什么态度 is a challenge, and 服务态度 is what you complain about at a shop.
- **胖子** [pàng zi] *fat person* · lost in translation  
  A fat person, said bluntly. Friends may call you 胖子 as a plain nickname, and nobody thinks it's as rude as it would sound elsewhere.
- **形象** [xíng xiàng] *image* · lost in translation  
  Your image. 注意形象 means mind how you look, and you hear it whenever someone is being sloppy in public.
- **资本主义** [zī běn zhǔ yì] *capitalism* · cultural context  
  Capitalism. You learned at school that socialism would replace it, and now you joke about your boss being a 资本家.
- **至于** [zhì yú] *to go so far as to* · lost in translation  
  To go so far as to. 至于吗 is what you say when someone overreacts, meaning is it really worth all that.
- **地下** [dì xià] *covert; clandestine* · cultural context  
  Covert. 地下党 makes you think of wartime spy dramas, and 地下恋情 is a romance you keep hidden.
- **进步** [jìn bù] *progress; improvement* · cultural context  
  Progress, and in a workplace a career word. 要求进步 means wanting promotion or Party membership, and you hear the official tone in it.
