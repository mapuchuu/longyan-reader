## Notes

- **成分** [chéng fèn] *one's social status* · cultural context  
  Class background as assigned under Mao: a 地主 (landlord) or 贫农 (poor peasant) label shaped schooling, jobs and safety. 成分不好 meant a bad class label.
- **伟大** [wěi dà] *huge; great; grand; worthy of the greatest admiration; important (contribution etc)* · lost in translation  
  Heavily official in tone: 伟大的祖国, 伟大领袖. Not used for everyday praise, where 太好了 or 了不起 fit better.
- **机制** [jī zhì] *mechanism* · lost in translation  
  In policy writing, a set of rules and procedures: 长效机制 ("long-term mechanism"), 激励机制 ("incentive system").
- **村委会** [cūn wěi huì] *village committee* · cultural context  
  Officially a self-governing body elected by villagers, not a level of government. It works alongside the village Party branch, whose secretary usually outranks its head.
- **考察** [kǎo chá] *to inspect* · lost in translation  
  Often an official tour: 领导到基层考察 ("leaders inspect the grassroots"). Also used for vetting a cadre before promotion: 考察干部.
- **观念** [guān niàn] *notion* · lost in translation  
  Often a group's or generation's attitudes: 传统观念 ("traditional attitudes"), 观念落后 ("outdated thinking").
- **营养** [yíng yǎng] *nutrition* · lost in translation  
  Also figurative online: 没营养的话题 is a vacuous topic, 有营养 means worth reading or watching.
- **行者** [xíng zhě] *itinerant monk* · cultural context  
  In Journey to the West, Sun Wukong is often called 行者 or 孙行者 as Tang Sanzang's disciple.
- **本人** [běn rén] *I; me; myself* · lost in translation  
  A formal or mock-formal "I", common in notices, statements and online posts: 本人郑重声明 ("I hereby solemnly declare").
- **本人** [běn rén] *oneself; yourself; himself; herself; the person concerned* · lost in translation  
  On forms and at service counters, the person in question acting in person: 须本人到场办理 ("must be handled in person").
- **外面** [wài miàn] *outside (also pr. [wai4 mian5] for this sense)* · lost in translation  
  In speech, 他在外面有人 means he has a lover outside his marriage. 在外面吃 means eating out.
- **桃花** [táo huā] *(fig.) love affair* · cultural context  
  Mostly in 桃花运 ("luck in love"), a term from fortune-telling. 烂桃花 are unwanted or troublesome romances.
- **少年** [shào nián] *youngster* · lost in translation  
  Usually someone in their early to mid teens, younger than a 青年. Also in 少年犯 ("juvenile offender") and 少年宫, a children's activity centre.
- **出席** [chū xí] *to attend* · lost in translation  
  Formal; news reports use it for officials and guests attending meetings and ceremonies. Friends 参加 a party rather than 出席 it.
- **理想** [lǐ xiǎng] *an ideal* · lost in translation  
  Often a career goal: 你的理想是什么？ asks children what they want to be when they grow up.
- **理想** [lǐ xiǎng] *ideal* · lost in translation  
  不太理想 is a soft way of saying results were poor: 考试成绩不太理想.
- **温度** [wēn dù] *temperature* · lost in translation  
  In slogans and ads, 有温度 means humane or caring: 有温度的服务 ("service with a human touch").
- **团结** [tuán jié] *unity; solidarity* · cultural context  
  A core word in official language: 民族团结 ("ethnic unity") and 安定团结 ("stability and unity") are fixed slogans.
- **省长** [shěng zhǎng] *governor of a province* · cultural context  
  Formally chosen by the provincial people's congress, and ranks below the provincial Party secretary.
- **出发** [chū fā] *(fig.) to proceed from (a particular standpoint or basis)* · lost in translation  
  A staple of official writing: 从实际出发 ("proceed from actual conditions"), 从群众利益出发.
- **民间** [mín jiān] *non-governmental* · lost in translation  
  Marks what is private or unofficial as opposed to state-run: 民间资本 ("private capital"), 民间借贷 ("informal lending").
- **点头** [diǎn tóu] *to nod* · lost in translation  
  Also means giving approval: 领导点头了 ("the boss signed off"). 点头之交 is a nodding acquaintance.
- **改造** [gǎi zào] *to remold* · cultural context  
  In PRC political language, remaking people's thinking: 思想改造 ("thought reform") and 劳动改造 ("reform through labour"), shortened to 劳改.
- **移动** [yí dòng] *mobile* · cultural context  
  Also short for 中国移动, China Mobile, the largest mobile carrier: 移动卡 is a China Mobile SIM. 移动支付 is paying by phone.
- **街道** [jiē dào] *subdistrict* · cultural context  
  The lowest level of city government, below the district. 街道办 handles local paperwork and services, with 居委会 working under its guidance.
- **一口** [yī kǒu] *flatly (deny, admit and so on)* · lost in translation  
  Usually in 一口咬定, to insist on a claim and refuse to budge, and 一口否认, to deny outright.
- **回头** [huí tóu] *later; by and by* · lost in translation  
  Common in speech: 回头见 ("see you later"), 回头再说 ("let's deal with it later"), which can also mean putting something off for good.
- **战士** [zhàn shì] *fighter* · lost in translation  
  Official language extends it to anyone praised as fighting for a cause: 白衣战士 for medical workers, 抗疫战士 during the pandemic.
- **自治** [zì zhì] *autonomy* · cultural context  
  In China, attached to areas with large ethnic minority populations: 自治区, 自治州 and 自治县, such as the Tibet Autonomous Region.
- **开发区** [kāi fā qū] *development zone* · cultural context  
  Zones set up by governments since the 1980s to draw investment with tax breaks and cheap land, often on a city's edge: 经济技术开发区.
- **县级** [xiàn jí] *county level* · cultural context  
  A rung in China's administrative ladder. A 县级市 is a city ranked as a county, below prefecture-level cities.
- **公斤** [gōng jīn] *kilogram (kg)* · cultural context  
  Used in official and technical contexts. At markets and in everyday talk weights are given in 斤, half a 公斤.
- **浩浩荡荡** [hào hào dàng dàng] *(idiom) grandiose; majestic* · lost in translation  
  Describes a vast mass on the move, such as a crowd, army or river: 浩浩荡荡的队伍 ("a huge procession streaming along").
- **独立自主** [dú lì zì zhǔ] *independent and autonomous (idiom); self-determination* · cultural context  
  A core principle in PRC official language, as in 独立自主的和平外交政策, the "independent foreign policy of peace".
- **流芳百世** [liú fāng bǎi shì] *(of one's name, reputation etc) to be immortalized (idiom)* · lost in translation  
  Formal praise for historical figures, often set against its opposite 遗臭万年, "to leave a name that stinks for ten thousand years".
- **咬牙切齿** [yǎo yá qiè chǐ] *(idiom) to gnash one's teeth; to fume with rage* · lost in translation  
  Usually expresses bitter hatred rather than passing anger: 恨得咬牙切齿 ("to hate someone with clenched teeth").
- **不动声色** [bù dòng shēng sè] *not a word or movement (idiom); remaining calm and collected* · lost in translation  
  Often describes someone acting while hiding their intentions: 不动声色地观察 means watching closely without giving anything away.
- **摸金校尉** [mō jīn xiào wèi] *(idiom) military title said to have existed in the Han dynasty, allegedly granted to officials tasked with retrieving gold from tombs to fund military campaigns; (in popular fiction) tomb raider* · cultural context  
  Best known today from the tomb-raiding novel series 鬼吹灯 (Ghost Blows Out the Light), which made it a popular nickname for grave robbers.
- **当家作主** [dāng jiā zuò zhǔ] *to be the master of one's own affairs* · cultural context  
  A key phrase of PRC political language: 人民当家作主 ("the people are masters of the country") describes the official basis of the state.
- **全心全意** [quán xīn quán yì] *(idiom) heart and soul; wholeheartedly* · cultural context  
  Strongly linked to Mao's slogan 全心全意为人民服务, "serve the people wholeheartedly", still the stated purpose of the Communist Party.
- **得天独厚** [dé tiān dú hòu] *blessed by heaven (idiom)* · lost in translation  
  Mostly said of places and their natural conditions: 得天独厚的地理位置. A stock phrase in tourism and investment promotion.
- **独树一帜** [dú shù yī zhì] *to stand out* · lost in translation  
  Praise for a distinctive style or approach, not a sign of being a loner: 他的画风独树一帜 ("his painting style is one of a kind").
- **耀武扬威** [yào wǔ yáng wēi] *to show off one's military strength (idiom); to strut around* · lost in translation  
  Always disapproving: showing off power to intimidate others, said of bullies, petty officials or an occupying army.
- **出其不意** [chū qí bù yì] *to do sth when least expected (idiom); to catch sb off guard* · cultural context  
  From the Art of War (孙子兵法): 攻其无备，出其不意, "attack where they are unprepared, appear where they do not expect".
- **马到成功** [mǎ dào chéng gōng] *to win instant success (idiom)* · lost in translation  
  Mostly heard as a good wish before an exam, contest or new venture: 祝你马到成功！ ("wishing you swift success!").
- **不以为然** [bù yǐ wéi rán] *not to accept as correct (idiom); to object* · lost in translation  
  Means disagreeing or disapproving, and is often misused for 不以为意, "not taking something seriously".
- **垂帘听政** [chuí lián tīng zhèng] *lit. to govern from behind the curtain* · cultural context

- **嚣张气焰** [xiāo zhāng qì yàn] *(idiom) overweening attitude; threatening manner* · lost in translation  
  A stock phrase of police reports and official rhetoric, usually as the target of a crackdown: 打击犯罪分子的嚣张气焰.
- **新婚燕尔** [xīn hūn yàn ěr] *(idiom) to experience the joy of being newly married* · cultural context  
  From the Book of Songs (诗经), where the line 宴尔新昏 is a rejected wife's lament about her husband's new bride. Now simply a happy phrase for newlyweds.
- **人杰地灵** [rén jié dì líng] *illustrious hero, spirit of the place (idiom); a place derives reflected glory from an illustrious son* · cultural context
