## Notes

- **阶级** [jiē jí] *(social) class* · cultural context  
  Social class. In the Mao years your family's 阶级 could decide your whole life, and today people complain online about 阶级固化, classes that no longer move.
- **红色** [hóng sè] *revolutionary* · cultural context  
  Revolutionary. 红色旅游 takes you to old Communist bases like Yan'an, and 红色歌曲 are the songs your parents' generation can still sing by heart.
- **路线** [lù xiàn] *political line (e.g. right revisionist road)* · cultural context  
  A political line. You think of the 路线斗争 of the Mao era, when the wrong line could end a career or worse.
- **文件** [wén jiàn] *document* · cultural context  
  A document, and in offices an instruction from above. 上面下了文件 means it's decided now, and a 红头文件 carries real weight.
- **录取** [lù qǔ] *to admit (a student)* · cultural context  
  To be admitted, usually to a university. A 录取通知书 is a letter your whole family celebrates, and relatives call to ask where you got in.
- **伸手** [shēn shǒu] *(fig.) to beg* · lost in translation  
  To hold out a hand for something. 伸手要钱 means asking for money you haven't earned, and it makes the person sound shameless.
- **配合** [pèi hé] *to cooperate* · lost in translation  
  To work together. Two people 配合得好 when they mesh well, while 请配合 from an official sounds polite and isn't really a request.
- **打击** [dǎ jī] *(psychological) shock* · lost in translation  
  A blow to your confidence. 别打击我 means don't discourage me, and you say it half joking when someone doubts you.
