# 1.1 release notes (draft)

Not published. 1.1 rather than 1.0.1, since it adds features.

**Add to Anki.** With Anki open and AnkiConnect installed, cards get an Add to Anki button. It adds the word with its pinyin, the meaning it had in your sentence, the sentence, and the note. Phrases go in with their translation. Pick the deck under Anki Deck in the menu. Nothing leaves your Mac.

**Tone colors.** Appearance ▸ Tone Colors now has Standard (Pleco's red, green, blue, purple), Dummitt (red, orange, green, blue, as on MDBG), Custom and Off. Edit Custom Colors… picks your own.

**Fixes**
- The pin button sits level with the others.
- Sense numbers of 10 and up stay on one line.
- A word with a reading listed twice in the dictionary shows it once.
- A selection of only punctuation no longer shows an empty Words section.
- Notes no longer show a double space after Chinese punctuation.
- Translations no longer show asterisks around book titles.
- 长 in 长得 and 行 in 不行 get the right meaning when clicked alone.
- A long word no longer pushes the buttons up.
- VoiceOver reads out a card when it appears, and the chosen meaning is marked selected.
- With Reduce Motion on, cards fade in without moving.
