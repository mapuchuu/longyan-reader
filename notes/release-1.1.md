Cards can now go straight into Anki, and the tone colors have more choices.

**New**

- Add to Anki. With Anki open and the AnkiConnect add-on installed, cards get an Add to Anki button. It adds the word with its pinyin, the meaning it had in your sentence, the sentence and the note. Phrases go in with their translation. Pick the deck under Anki Deck in the menu. Nothing leaves your Mac.
- Tone colors. Appearance ▸ Tone Colors has Standard (red, green, blue, purple), Dummitt (red, orange, green, blue), Custom and Off. Edit Custom Colors… lets you pick your own.

**Better**

- VoiceOver reads out a card when it appears, and marks the chosen meaning as selected.
- With Reduce Motion on, cards fade in without moving.
- 长 in 长得 and 行 in 不行 get the right meaning when clicked on their own.

**Fixed**

- The pin button sits level with the others.
- Sense numbers of 10 and up stay on one line.
- A reading listed twice in the dictionary shows once.
- A selection of only punctuation no longer shows an empty Words section.
- Notes no longer show a double space after Chinese punctuation.
- Translations no longer show asterisks around book titles.
- A long word no longer pushes the card's buttons up.
