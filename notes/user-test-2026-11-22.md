# User test, 22 November 2026

The usage simulation now covers Add to Anki and tone colors: 148 checks, all passing. Two agents also drew 67 cards (41 word, 26 phrase) in light and dark across the tone schemes and looked for problems. Nothing was clipped or overlapping, and the header never hit the buttons.

None of these comes from the Anki or tone color work. The ones marked fixed were fixed later the same day.

## Cards

- Fixed: a sense number of 10 or more wrapped onto two lines (你好，很高兴认识你。 on 好). The number column now fits the longest number.
- Fixed: the same reading heading could show twice (好: a second hǎo group after hào). Each reading's meanings now sit together under one heading, keeping their dictionary numbers.
- 请把门关上。 on 把 says it's part of 把门, "to keep guard on a gate".
- Fixed: a selection of only punctuation (？？！) showed a Words header with nothing under it.
- When a long word's pinyin wraps under it (中华人民共和国), the button row sits 4 pt higher than on other cards.
- Opened phrase rows label some meanings with a part of speech and not others (画蛇添足, 会议). 一举两得 is labelled n.
- Asking to open 在乎 in 不在乎 opens no row, since the sentence splits it as 不在乎.
- Fixed: a double space after 吃饭了吗？ in its cultural note. A full-width mark carries its own space, so the typed space after it is dropped when notes are shown (27 notes had one). The model still gets the note as written.

## Senses and translations

- 他长得很高。 on 长 alone picks cháng, long.
- 你这样做不行。 on 行 gives "to do", not "all right".
- 吃瓜群众's headline is "neologism c. 2016".
- Names shrink to one character: 小明 shows 小, 王小二 shows 王.
- 我明天要用iPhone开一个Zoom会议，讨论API的问题。 drops API and makes up "new features"; 开 is "to turn on".
- 他说："我读过《红楼梦》……但没读完。" shows `*Dream of the Red Chamber*` with the asterisks, ends on a comma, and the 红楼梦 gloss ends in a stray …
- 你别躺平了，内卷也没用，摆烂吧。 translates 内卷 as trying to "involve" yourself (the row says rat race).
- Row glosses that miss the context: 张 with 票 as "measure word for votes", 好 as "so" when the translation says "Great!", 项目 "item" and 工作 "task".

## Add to Anki, from a code review

All fixed, each with a check in the simulation:
- A note edited in Anki (a trailing <br>) wasn't found, so the button said "duplicate". It now finds the note the way Anki compares duplicates, without formatting.
- A second warning was cleared at once by the first one's timer.
- A phrase whose translation failed sat on "Adding…" for 20 s and then gave up silently. Now it says there's no translation to add.
- The menu showed the previous check's answer, so Anki Deck was missing just after Anki opened. The open menu now catches up.
- A deck renamed or deleted in Anki stays listed and checked, since the next card makes it again.

## Second pass, same day

Fixed:
- 吃瓜群众's headline was "neologism c. 2016". CC-CEDICT lists that dating label as a sense of its own (the only one like it), so senses that are only a dating label are skipped.
- 长 clicked in 他长得很高 read cháng and 行 in 你这样做不行 read "to do". Two cues fix them (长得 → to grow, 不行 → all right). A single character now takes a cue only if the word it belongs to fits inside it, so the 行 of 不行动 isn't caught.
- Translations showed markdown, `*Dream of the Red Chamber*`. Asterisk and underscore emphasis is taken out unless the Chinese had those marks. A sentence ending in 。 no longer ends its English on a comma.
- 红楼梦's gloss ended in a stray "…" where only a bracket was cut (75 glosses in the dictionary change, for example 花木兰).
- When a long word shrank to fit (中华人民共和国), the buttons moved up 4 pt. They now stay where every card has them.
- AnkiConnect replies are capped (1 MB, 200 decks, a short message), since anything can answer on that port. The privacy page now says each card asks Anki whether its word is already in the deck.
- VoiceOver: a card is announced when it appears (word, pinyin, meaning). The chosen meaning is marked selected and its checkmark is hidden.
- Reduce Motion: cards fade in without growing or drifting, and the card's motion is a short ease.

Need a decision:
- **把 and 被.** In 请把门关上, clicking 把 shows "Part of 把门" (to keep guard). The rule that drops words glued to 把/被 applies only when you click the other character, so clicking 握 in 把握 also loses "Part of 把握". Dropping every 把/被 word fixes 请把门 but loses 把握 when you click 把. A short list of glued words (把门) is the narrow fix.
- **Names.** 小明 shows 小 "young", and 王小二 shows "Part of 小二: waiter". There's no name handling; surname senses are dropped. Apple's NLTagger finds these names (小明, 王小二, 张伟, 李华, 小红), but it also tags 高明 and 王子, so dictionary words would be skipped. The card could say "Part of 小明 · a name". How it should look is your call.
- **"API" dropped from a translation.** The text reaches the model; the model drops it. Not fixable without prompt work.
- **Contrast.** The resting button glyphs (1.6:1 light, 2.7:1 dark) and sense numbers are below the 3:1 for controls, and secondary text is 3.1:1 in light mode (4.5:1 is the bar). The quiet look is deliberate, so this is a design choice.
- **Keyboard and VoiceOver use of the card.** The card never takes focus, so its buttons can't be reached from the keyboard; Esc doesn't close a pinned card; Close is hidden until hovered. A second ⌃⌥L could focus the card.
- **Buttons don't grow with zoom.** The text does; the 12 pt glyphs and the close badge don't.
- **Ollama backend.** When the model file is missing, the app talks to whatever answers on port 11434 and lists the model, and sends it the passage. Since Ollama's own copy of the model is read directly anyway, this HTTP path could be dev-only.

Checks on the second pass: `--check-data` clean; `scripts/evals.sh` no regressions (phrase 83/95 from 82, phrase-notes 82/90 from 81, real-T 49/52 from 48); usage simulation passes everything but the 11 drags, which the control fails too. The simulation used to stop the Anki stand-in with `pkill -f`, which also killed any shell naming the script; it now stops it by its port.
