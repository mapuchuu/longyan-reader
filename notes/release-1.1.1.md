Better meanings for common words.

**Fixed**

- Some everyday words were missing their most common meaning, so a card could show the wrong one. 不想 now reads "don't want to", not "unexpectedly". 一家 in 一家公司 is "a; one", not "group". 到底 in a question is "on earth". 一声, 一道, 一则, 不要, 西方, 海里, 山下, 落下 and about 20 more get their everyday meaning too. The dictionary's other meanings are still there and are picked when the sentence calls for them.
- 小林 reads "Xiao Lin" instead of "Kobayashi".
